About Tenner

Your top ten, every hour.

Tenner shows what the UK is watching right now on Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video, HBO Max and Paramount+. One page, five services, fresh every hour.

From the streamers to your screen.

Every chart goes through the same four stages.

  1. 1

    Popular right now

    TMDB measures what millions of people are watching, searching for and adding to their lists, and turns it into a popularity score for every film and show.

  2. 2

    Matched to UK services

    Where-to-watch data from JustWatch, shared through TMDB, shows which of those titles are streaming on each of the five services in the UK right now.

  3. 3

    Rebuilt every hour

    At seven minutes past every hour Tenner fetches the latest figures, builds a fresh chart for every service and publishes it straight away.

  4. 4

    On your screen

    You get the top ten for every service in one place, with posters, ratings and a link to where each title is streaming.

What the numbers mean.

A few things worth knowing when you read the chart.

  • Rank

    Ordered by TMDB popularity among titles on that service in the UK. It's a strong guide to what people are watching, though not official viewing figures.

  • Rating

    The average score from TMDB members, out of 10. It only shows once at least 20 people have rated a title.

  • Where to watch

    Included in a subscription, free, or free with ads. Titles you can only rent or buy are left out.

  • New this week

    Releases from the last and next seven days, from Watchmode. The dates are mostly US dates, so a few UK ones can differ.

  • Updated

    The green pill shows how long ago the chart was built. Tap it to check for a newer one.
