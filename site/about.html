About Tenner

Your top ten, every hour.

Tenner shows what the UK is watching right now on Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video, HBO Max and Paramount+. One page, five services, refreshed every hour.

Made for watching.

Four things worth knowing about Tenner.

  1. Popular right now

    Tenner measures what millions of people are watching, searching for and adding to their lists, and turns it into a popularity score for every film and show.

  2. New features every week

    Tenner is in its early days, and we're busy adding something new every week. Keep checking back to see what's arrived.

  3. Updated every hour

    Every hour, Tenner checks in with TMDB and JustWatch for the latest numbers, so you always see what the UK is watching and rating right now.

  4. Made for your phone

    Tenner is designed phone first, so the best trending films and shows are always a tap away, wherever you are.

Popularity, posters and where-to-watch data from TMDB (powered by JustWatch). New releases from Watchmode.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Tenner isn't connected to Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video, HBO Max or Paramount+.
