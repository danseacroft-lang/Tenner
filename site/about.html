Questions and answers.

What is Tenner?

Tenner shows the UK's top 10 shows and films on Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video, HBO Max, Paramount+ and Apple TV, all in one place. You can look at every service together or one at a time, and browse everything popular on them.

How is the top 10 worked out?

We use popularity from TMDB, which reflects what people are watching, searching for, rating and adding to their lists, and keep only the titles streaming on each service in the UK. It's a strong guide to what's popular, though not official viewing figures.

How often is it updated?

Every hour. The time of the last update is shown at the top of the Top 10 page, and you can tap it to check for a newer chart.

Why isn't a film I'm watching on the list?

The Top 10 only counts titles included with a subscription. Brand-new films are often only available to buy or rent at first. Search for one and its sheet shows where to rent or buy it, and the price where we have it.

Is Tenner free?

Yes, completely. There are no adverts and nothing to pay for.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without one, and your Watchlist and settings are kept on your device. An account is optional: sign in (with a passkey, Google or an email and password) and your Watchlist, progress and settings stay in step on every phone and computer you use.

How do Watchlist alerts work?

Turn them on from your Watchlist or in Settings, and Tenner sends a notification when something you've saved arrives on a service, gets a new episode, or is about to leave. On an iPhone or iPad, add Tenner to your Home Screen first (Share, then Add to Home Screen) and open it from there.

Is Tenner part of Netflix, Disney+ or the other services?

No. Tenner is independent. The services' names and logos belong to them. Popularity, artwork and where-to-watch details come from TMDB and JustWatch, and new releases from Watchmode.

Can I suggest a feature?

Please do. We read everything, and plenty of Tenner started as someone's idea. Send us a message.
