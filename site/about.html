        Privacy Policy

        Questions

        How is the top 10 worked out?

        We use popularity from TMDB, which reflects what people are watching, searching for, rating and adding to their lists, and keep only the titles streaming on each service in the UK. It’s a strong guide to what’s popular, though not official viewing figures.

        Do I need an account?

        No. Everything works without one, and your Watchlist and settings are kept on your device. Sign in (with a passkey, Google or an email and password) and they stay in step on every phone and computer you use.

        Is Tenner part of Netflix or the other services?

        No. Tenner is independently run, with no big company behind it. The services’ names and logos belong to them. Popularity, artwork and where-to-watch details come from TMDB and JustWatch, and new releases from Watchmode.

        Can I suggest a feature?

        Please do. We read everything, and plenty of Tenner started as someone’s idea. Send us a message.
