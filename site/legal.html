Privacy Policy

1. Overview

Tenner (“we”, “us”, “our”) is a website that shows the UK’s most popular shows and films on Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video, HBO Max and Paramount+, updated every hour. This policy explains what information is involved when you visit the site and what choices you have.

The short version: unless you choose to create an account, we don’t collect or store any personal information about you. There’s no advertising and no tracking. Accounts are optional, and so are messages and Watchlist alerts; each is explained below.

2. Information We Collect

We don’t collect personal information when you visit the site. In particular, the site does not use:

  • analytics or visitor-tracking tools;
  • advertising or advertising networks;
  • cookies of any kind.

We don’t ask for your location. The charts are for the UK as a whole.

If you send us a message. The Contact page’s form sends your message, your email address, the type of enquiry you choose and your name, if you add it, to us as an email, through our search service on Cloudflare. It isn’t stored there: it’s passed straight on to our inbox. If you email us at support@tenner.stream instead, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to read and reply to you. We don’t add you to any mailing list or share your details with anyone. Emails are kept only as long as needed to deal with your message, and you can ask us to delete them at any time. Our email provider processes messages under its own privacy policy.

If you turn on Watchlist alerts. Alerts are off unless you switch them on. When you do, your browser gives Tenner a push address for this device (made by your browser’s own notification service, such as Apple’s, Google’s or Mozilla’s), and Tenner keeps it on Cloudflare together with the list of titles on your Watchlist (their TMDB numbers only), so it can tell you when one of them arrives on a service or gets a new season. Nothing else is kept: no name, email address or location. The list is updated when your Watchlist changes, and both are deleted when you turn alerts off, or when your browser tells us the address no longer works. Alerts are sent through your browser’s notification service and are encrypted so that only your device can read them.

If you create an account. Accounts are optional; Tenner works the same without one. If you make one, we keep:

  • Your name, as you typed it (or your first name from Google), and the colour you choose for your initial;
  • How you sign in: the public half of each passkey you add (the private half never leaves your device or password manager), and, if you use Google, Google’s number for your account, your email address and your profile picture’s address, so we can recognise you and show which Google account is linked;
  • What you keep in Tenner: your Watchlist, where you’re up to in shows, your services and your settings, so they’re the same on every device you sign in on;
  • Where you’re signed in: the kind of device (such as “iPhone”), when you signed in and when it was last used. The sign-in itself is a random code kept on your device; we store only a scrambled copy of it;
  • Your shared list link, if you turn sharing on. Anyone with the link can see your name, picture and Watchlist until you turn it off.

This is kept in a database run by Cloudflare. We use it only to run your account. You can see it on the Account page, sign out of any device, and delete your account there at any time, which removes everything above straight away. Accounts don’t use cookies.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

To make the site work better, your browser keeps a few things on your own device:

  • Your theme: if you pick Auto or Day instead of Night.
  • Shows, Films or Both: which charts you last chose to see.
  • An offline copy: the site’s pages, its latest charts and some recent posters, so Tenner opens quickly and still works without a connection (especially if you add it to your home screen).
  • Your Watchlist: the titles you save, with their names and posters. It stays in this browser, on this device, unless you sign in to an account (which keeps it in step on your devices), or carry it across yourself with Move to another device in Settings.
  • My services: the streaming services you’ve told Tenner you use, if you choose them, so it can show you a Top 10 of just those.
  • Your settings: the choices you make on the Settings page, such as which page Tenner opens on.
  • Your progress: the season and episode you’re up to in shows you’re watching, if you set it. Like your Watchlist, it stays on this device.
  • The privacy note: that you’ve pressed Got it, so the note about cookies isn’t shown again.

Your filters and searches on Browse live only in the page’s address, so you can bookmark or share them; they aren’t saved anywhere. Move to another device (in Settings) puts your Watchlist, progress and services into a link or QR code that only you see; nothing is uploaded. Apart from Watchlist alerts and your account, if you choose them (above), none of this is ever sent to us or anyone else. You can remove it at any time by clearing your browser’s data for this site.

4. Third-Party Services

Like every website, Tenner relies on a few outside services. When your browser connects to them, they receive the technical details that any web request carries, such as your IP address and browser type. Each handles that information under its own privacy policy:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and its hourly charts. GitHub may log visitors’ IP addresses for security and to keep the service running. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.
  • TMDB (The Movie Database) supplies the posters, artwork and service logos, which your browser loads directly from TMDB’s image servers. See TMDB’s Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare runs Tenner’s search service. When you search all films and shows, search Cast & Crew or open a cast list, what you typed or opened is passed to TMDB to find the answer. It also passes on messages from the Contact page to our inbox, keeps the push address and Watchlist of anyone who turns on alerts, and holds accounts for anyone who makes one (see section 2). Nothing else is stored against you. See Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy.
  • Google provides Sign in with Google. Its button loads from Google only on the Account page, and only Google’s sign-in is involved: if you use it, Google tells us your account number, name, email address and picture. See Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube plays trailers, but only when you press Trailer on a title. Tenner uses YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), and nothing loads from YouTube until you press play. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

The charts and where-to-watch details are gathered every hour by our own automated process from TMDB (with where-to-watch data from JustWatch), Netflix’s published Top 10 and Watchmode. Your browser doesn’t contact JustWatch, Netflix or Watchmode while you use Tenner. Watchmode also supplies the Coming soon and Leaving soon dates and the rent and buy prices. If you follow a link to Netflix, TMDB or another site, that site’s own policies apply.

5. Cookies

Tenner doesn’t set any cookies, and doesn’t use any third-party cookies. The things described in section 3 are kept in your browser’s local storage, not in cookies, and never leave your device.

6. Links to Other Websites

Tenner links to other websites, such as Netflix and TMDB, so you can watch or read more about a title. We aren’t responsible for the content or privacy practices of those sites; please check their own privacy policies.

7. Children

Tenner is a general-audience site. We don’t collect personal information from anyone, including children. Some of the titles shown may not be suitable for younger viewers; check each service’s age ratings.

8. Your Rights

Under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018), you have rights over any personal data we hold about you, including the right to see it, correct it or have it deleted. As we don’t collect personal data, this will normally only apply to emails you send us. If you have concerns, you can also contact the Information Commissioner’s Office.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we change how Tenner handles information, we’ll update this policy and the date at the top before the change takes effect.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Get in touch on the Contact page or email support@tenner.stream.
